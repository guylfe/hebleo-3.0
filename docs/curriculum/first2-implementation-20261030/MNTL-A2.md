Learn — exact text and embedded interactions
Screen 1: Four shapes, four sounds · MNTL-A2.learn.mem-nun
LEARNER TEXT:
מ represents /m/, the sound at the start of mountain. Its name is Mem.
נ represents /n/, the sound at the start of nose. Its name is Nun.
ת represents /t/, as at the start of tripping. Its name is Tav.
ל represents /l/, as at the start of ladle. Its name is Lamed.
Listen to each sound and look at its letter. The mountain-climber, nose, tripping and ladle pictures connect each printed shape to a familiar word that begins with its sound. You do not need to memorise the letter names yet.
[AUTHOR INSTRUCTION] AUDIO MNTL-M, MNTL-N, MNTL-T and MNTL-L: human recordings of /m/, /n/, /t/ and /l/. Play individually on request, without an added vowel. Present all four on this page.

Apply — complete exercise set
MNTL-A2.apply.four-sounds — matching
LEARNER PROMPT: Match each sound to its printed letter.
SUPPLIED MATERIAL: Left-hand prompts: /m/; /n/; /t/; /l/
OPTIONS / TOKEN POOL:
A. ת
B. ל
C. מ
D. נ
ANSWER / ACCEPTED MODEL: /m/ → מ | /n/ → נ | /t/ → ת | /l/ → ל
FEEDBACK: מ = /m/, נ = /n/, ת = /t/, ל = /l/. Replay a missed sound, then retry it without the mnemonic.
TARGETS: מ; נ; ת; ל

FEEDBACK: Reveal the four individual sound buttons and compare one at a time. This is self-check, not automatic pronunciation scoring.
