The revision preserves all 1069 existing target entries, relocates 75 entries and adds 18, for 1087 targets across 114 lessons. The catalog includes 528 example sentences. Counts are entries/meaning targets, not unique Hebrew lemmas.

## Principles and resulting decisions

- **Improve — usefulness:** everyday requests, communication, people, time and navigation move earlier when their letters and a manageable form model permit it.
- **Preserve — old-to-new connections:** earlier verb vocabulary is explicitly modelled in the currently usable form. Systematic binyan/conjugation lessons retrieve those familiar verbs later. A known root is not evidence of a known sense.
- **Preserve — spacing:** teaching-purpose families have at least two intervening lessons. Seventeen native row moves were prepared to preserve comment anchors. Familiar grammar reuse within vocabulary scenes does not count as a new grammar lesson.
- **Improve — manageable load:** displaced items are retained in later suitable scenes. Not every rare or specialist noun in the supplied candidate document becomes a compulsory early target.
- **Improve — identity:** readable content IDs are independent of position. A reordered lesson keeps its ID, source links and aliases.
