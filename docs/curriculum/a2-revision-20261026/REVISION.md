## Decisions and rationale

- **Preserve:** integrated reading and practical sentence use, familiar-to-new connections, native audio, curated Nikkud and human authority over teaching. The original A route remains intact.
- **Improve — questions once:** move the existing QUESTIONS-BASIC lesson to position 7. There is no later question-word grammar unit. Other question words enter as vocabulary where useful replies are possible. Noun plurals use the freed later position. The separate pronoun unit is absorbed into the opening meaningful uses, giving 116 lessons.
- **Improve — pronouns by person:** אני with self-introduction at 6; אתה/את/אתם/אתן with addressing people at 7; הוא/היא/הם/הן with talking about people at 8. Explain person, gender and number explicitly. Plural pronouns do not require plural verb formation yet. אנחנו retains its later source placement; ח is unavailable in the opening.
- **Improve — vowel timing:** /o/ joins a/e/i and silent sheva, staged within the first vowel lesson. לא then supports correction in the first nominal sentences. ו links to the familiar /o/ and supplies /u/. Kubutz waits for pointed material that actually uses it. The former second vowel lesson becomes integration with new vocabulary only.
- **Improve — letter payoff:** מנתל → אהי → ו → שזד → ברצ → כפג → חע → קסט. ד supports תודה and person-word gender before the first verbs. ר/צ then complete רוצה. Each letter lesson still has exactly two intervening lessons.
- **Improve — gender before verbs:** זה/זאת at 12 and person-word gender at 13 form the agreed connected two-part introduction. First present at 15 supplies רוצה, מדבר/מדברת, לומד/לומדת and בא/באה; plural verbs return at 29. עברית waits until ע at 20; early מדבר can already answer who is speaking.
- **Improve — explain ל before לי:** ל־, לי and שלי enter at 16. Commands can use תן לי at 18, and יש לי follows at 19. Prefixes and pronouns are structural targets, excluded from additional vocabulary counts.
- **Improve — common actions:** הולך/הולכת at 17 anchors לך/לכי/לכו at 18; come, sit and give are staged with it. Wanted actions at 21 supply ללכת/לשבת/לרדת. Permission at 24 adds לקחת/לתת. New forms of known meanings are practice, not additional vocabulary entries.
- **Improve — practicability:** all 1,088 source entries remain allocated once. Ninety entries move between lessons. Column E now gives every entry a concrete use, alongside known or explicitly supplied forms. Columns F/G distinguish teacher citation forms from learner forms. A later past–present citation pair never silently licenses an early past-tense exercise.
- **Reconsider — one explicit load exception:** first self-introduction has three additional lexical items, היי/אמן/מנהל, plus structural אני/לא. The current requirement that every introduction be useful takes priority over adding unsuitable words to reach six. All other lessons from position 5 have 6–12 additional lexical meanings. This count is not a complete measure of cognitive load.

## What the practice check means

Each introduction has a model in vocabulary-practice.json and the proposed E column. Before sentences begin, picture/audio recognition is appropriate. Later, full sentences, natural short offers/replies, greetings and number-label exchanges count as usable communication. A question's reply must also be available. For example:
